# AgriSphere - Project Overview

## 🎯 Vision & Purpose
AgriSphere is designed to bridge the digital gap for farmers in Karnataka (with primary initial focus on Belagavi district). It unifies essential agricultural tools, daily market prices, weather forecasts, crop advisories, and government scheme information into one seamless digital experience.

## 🏆 Objectives
1. **Accessibility**: Intuitive, responsive dashboard tailored for easy navigation on mobile devices and desktop computers.
2. **Reliability**: Honest representation of sample vs live data, ensuring clear communication with users.
3. **Scalability**: Clean modular architecture supporting future integrations (APMC APIs, OpenWeatherMap, AI disease diagnosis models).
4. **Maintainability**: Clear separation of concerns among backend, frontend, and database team members.

## 🚩 Target User Persona
- **Primary User**: Ramesh Patil (Farmer, Belagavi, Karnataka)
- **Primary Need**: Quick daily check on APMC tomato and onion rates, weather warnings for field spraying, seed calculation for new crops, and drip irrigation subsidy applications.
